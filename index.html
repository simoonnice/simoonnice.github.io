Md Simoon Nice

I study how PFAS, microplastics and other emerging contaminants move through water and soil, and how we can treat them.

My research connects field sampling and laboratory characterization with environmental data analysis, from surface waters in Texas to coral reefs and farm soils in Bangladesh, and now PFAS adsorption and biosolid treatment in the Emerging Contaminants Research Lab at NJIT.

Research interests

A cross-section of where my work sits, from the water surface down into the soil. Each layer is a medium I sample, measure and model.

Surface water

Water and wastewater quality

Contaminant behavior, monitoring, treatment approaches and water-quality assessment across natural and engineered systems, including storm-driven bacterial extremes and green infrastructure.

Water column & interfaces

PFAS and emerging contaminants

Fate, transport and treatment of PFAS and other emerging contaminants: modified adsorbents for long- and short-chain PFAS, biosolid treatment to limit PFAS release to farmland, and reproducible handling of left-censored monitoring data.

Sediments & coasts

Microplastics and pollution

Occurrence, characterization, source apportionment, and ecological and human-health risk of microplastics, along with their interactions with heavy metals and other contaminants.

Soils

Soil carbon dynamics

Soil organic matter composition, lignocellulosic carbon, and management practices such as cover crops and compost that support resilient urban agricultural soils.

About

I'm interested in how contaminants move through environmental systems, how they interact with natural materials, and how science can turn that understanding into practical, sustainable solutions.

At Jashore University of Science and Technology I spent four years on microplastics, toxic elements and low-cost water treatment, work behind most of the articles listed below. At Texas Christian University I worked on a USDA-funded project on soil carbon in urban farms and on PFAS in the Trinity River.

I'm now pursuing a Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, where my work focuses on PFAS and emerging contaminants.

Research experience

Projects across emerging contaminants, water quality, soil carbon and environmental pollution, newest first.

  1. New Jersey Institute of TechnologyEmerging Contaminants Research Lab (ECRL)Graduate researcher, Aug 2026–present

    PFAS removal from water and biosolids

    Developing and evaluating modified adsorbent materials to improve PFAS removal from water, and investigating how long- and short-chain PFAS adsorb under different conditions to understand removal mechanisms. Also researching biosolid treatment processes that reduce PFAS release through biosolid management and agricultural land application, with laboratory-scale experiments aimed at practical use in water and wastewater treatment facilities.

  2. Texas Christian UniversityUSDA NIFA-funded project, 2024–2026

    Sustainable soil resource management on urban farms

    Research assistant on Sustainable Soil Resource Management and Produce Marketing on Resource-limited Urban Farms. Assessed soil carbon dynamics in managed urban farm soils under cover-crop and compost treatments, using TGA, GC-MS, Raman spectroscopy and a 2D-DTG mixing-ratio approach to characterize lignocellulosic carbon and its links to carbon storage and soil-water retention. Mentored a high school student who presented summer research at a conference.

  3. Texas Christian UniversityGraduate research, 2024–2026

    PFAS in the Trinity River

    Investigated PFAS occurrence in Trinity River surface waters through environmental sampling and analytical-data interpretation, including reproducible treatment of left-censored measurements below detection limits.

  4. Jashore University of Science and TechnologyGraduate research, Feb 2023–Jun 2024

    Microplastics across environmental matrices

    Explored microplastic pollution in water, sediment, soil, dust and food using SEM, FTIR and chemical digestion protocols, and led the research of six undergraduate thesis students. Research assistant on two JUST Research Cell projects: microplastics in urban outdoor and indoor environments, and a first study of microplastics in surface water, sediment and soil in Bangladesh. Led studies on microplastics in agricultural soil in Jashore, in protected ponds and harvested rainwater, and in aquaculture ponds.

  5. Asia Arsenic NetworkResearch assistant, Feb 2022–Jun 2024

    Toxic elements and environmental pollution

    Laboratory research on arsenic, heavy metals and other potentially toxic elements in soil, water and food, contributing to studies on their sources and ecological and human-health risks, and to adsorption research on removing heavy metals and dyes from wastewater.

  6. Jashore University of Science and TechnologyUndergraduate research, Jun 2020–Dec 2022

    Water treatment and soil amendments

    Assessed biochar and bio-nano composites as soil amendments and developed water and wastewater treatment methods, using FTIR, ICP-OES and AAS. Research assistant on two Ministry of Science and Technology projects: groundwater health risk in southwest Bangladesh using GIS, water quality indices and multivariate statistics (2021–2022), and methylene blue removal with jute stick charcoal (2019–2020). Contributed to studies on bio-nano composites for sustainable nutrient release and on poultry litter biochar as a soil amendment.

TRCELab

Technologies for Removing Anthropogenic Contaminants & Emerging pollutants

TRACE Lab is my personal research platform, focused on water treatment for PFAS, microplastics and other emerging contaminants: designing adsorbents that capture them, and understanding how they move through soil, water and living systems. It reflects a long-term goal of building an interdisciplinary research group around contaminant removal.
